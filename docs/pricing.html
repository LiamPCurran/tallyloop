Common questions

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan covers one active client with unlimited time tracking and three invoices a month. Upgrade only when you need more.

Can my clients pay online?

Yes. On Solo and Studio, every invoice has a pay-by-card button. Card processing fees are charged by the payment provider.

Can I import my existing time entries?

You can import time entries from a CSV file, which most time trackers can export.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Plans are month-to-month, and you can export all your data before you leave.
