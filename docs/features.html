Features built for solo freelancers

Three jobs, done well: track time, invoice it, get paid.

What's included

One-click timer

Track time from the browser or your phone. Forgot to start it? Add hours later in seconds.

Hours → invoice in two clicks

Pick a client and a date range. Tallyloop builds the invoice from your tracked time and your rates.

Polite payment reminders

Overdue invoices get friendly automatic reminders, so you don't have to write awkward emails.

Online card payments

Clients pay by card straight from the invoice. You see the payment the moment it lands.

Project budgets

Set hours or dollar budgets per project and get a heads-up at 80%, before you go over.

Tax-time exports

Export income and invoices by year or quarter as CSV or PDF for your accountant.

Free for your first client. No card needed.
